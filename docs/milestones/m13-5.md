## Development finding

An owned 1024x768 window journey found that keyboard Move could offer a false right-edge snap when its virtual caption grip began to the left of the screen. Signed movement deltas were correct, but the unsigned virtual grip was incorrectly treated as a real pointer position for snap proposals. M13.5 restricts automatic edge proposals to pointer movement. Keyboard Win+Left/Right remain explicit snap commands. The regression requires ten right-arrow steps to preserve exactly the expected normal rectangle, with no complementary-window overlay left open, followed by ordinary taskbar switching. The original failed candidate remains diagnostic evidence, not qualification.
