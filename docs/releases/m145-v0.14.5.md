The legacy console notice remains conservative; follow the named preview tasks in the current guide. Network/root labels do not establish network-share support.

Their required runtimes remain M14.10 work and all complete installer/use/save/restart/removal scenarios remain blocking M14.12 requirements. No target was removed. Firefox is download-only.
