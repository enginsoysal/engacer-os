## What changes

Settings → Appearance adds the optional Off-by-default Screensaver page. Choose one, five or ten actual idle minutes and the native Engacer logo, Clock or Blank style. Preview never saves; Apply persists a strict separate twenty-byte per-user record. Cancel, leaving the page or closing restores the saved choices, and a competing Settings window cannot replace another owner's draft.

The saver reuses the existing buffers, renderer, timer wakeups and physical input providers. It adds no worker or framebuffer. Reduced motion keeps the logo still; Clock uses the shared UTC service independently of the optional Clock app. Physical key holds and genuine input changes are tracked separately from translated keys and periodic zero-motion reports. Preview waits for its activating gesture to finish; wake consumes the entire first gesture before returning to retained windows and dirty work.

High contrast gains an explicit white Start perimeter and current Files-place boundary. Existing appearance, accessibility, Start, taskbar and optional-app choices keep their independent validated data. Every recipe defaults the saver to Off; no unsupported schema-3 preference field or system-file data-package target is offered.
