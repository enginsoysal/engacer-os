# Your first day with Engacer OS M14.8

**Development guide: M14.8 final qualification is pending.** The qualified public
download remains M14.7 until native tests and publication finish.

Start with the [matching VMware installation guide](install-m148.md). Finish the
wizard and restart into the installed desktop before trying application tasks.

Open My files or Documents. Click once to select; double-click or press Enter to
open. Back, Forward, Up and the address field navigate folders. Keep personal
documents separate from application installation files. Supported appearance
preferences remain in Settings; optional native apps are independently removable.

Open Windows Apps from Start to review an original MSI or EXE. The
[installer walkthrough](windows-m148.md) explains the first original EXE GUI
install/remove/reinstall profile and its required close-window step. Installation
does not establish that the installed reader or editor works. Use the console
and graphical-preview buttons for their separate supported 7-Zip tasks.

For supported MSI transactions, Installed apps offers removal and guided
recovery. Interrupted EXE records currently require inspection; do not treat MSI
recovery as automatic EXE rollback. Preserve the files and read Details.

Browser choices download an installer; they do not provide a working Firefox
browser yet. Read the [current stage and limits](../milestones/m14-8.md).
M14.9–M14.16 are the [approved future program](../milestones/m14-consolidated.md),
planned and not started. Development stops after M14.8 as requested by the owner.
