# Window controls in M12.4

M12.4 is qualified within M12-STAGE4-VM-v1.
[Install the matching media](install-m124.md), read the
[release scope and carry-forward limits](../releases/m124-v0.12.4.md), or use the
[handbook index](index.md) for the current tested download.

The app icon at the left of a title bar opens its window menu. At the right,
Minimize hides the window on the taskbar, Maximize fills the available desktop,
and Close closes it. A maximized window shows Restore in place of Maximize.
Point at a button to see its name. Pressing and releasing outside a button
cancels the click.

Drag empty title-bar space to move a window. Drag a border or corner to resize
it. Escape cancels a move or resize in progress. Double-clicking the title bar
maximizes or restores the window. Application minimum sizes still apply.

The active window has a stronger border and accent line. Click another window
to work in it. Selecting the desktop transfers keyboard ownership to the desktop;
Alt+F4 does not close a background app from there.

Use Tab to focus a title-bar control and Enter to activate it. Alt+Space opens
the window menu, including keyboard Move and Size. Arrow keys change position
or size; Enter keeps the change and Escape cancels it. Windows+Up maximizes;
Windows+Down restores or minimizes. Alt+Tab switches between open windows.

Closing an edited document asks you to review unsaved work. Choose Keep editing
to return to it, or explicitly Discard if you want to close without saving. A
file dialog must be completed or cancelled before its owner's title-bar controls
or window-arrangement shortcuts can be used. Other windows remain available.
This protects the operation rather than silently abandoning it.
