# Switch and arrange windows — M12.11

M12.11 passes native qualification. Use the [matching installation guide](install-m1211.md)
and official release assets; complete public delivery closes separately.

## Find another window

Hold **Alt** and press **Tab**. The list shows each app's icon and its real
document or folder. A number distinguishes two windows with the same name.
`[min]` identifies a minimized window; `[unsaved]` identifies unsaved editor work.
Press Tab again, Shift+Tab or the arrows to choose. Release Alt or press Enter
to bring the selected window forward. Click a row to select it directly.
**Escape** closes the list and keeps your current window.

## Place windows beside each other

1. Open the app icon at the left of a title bar, right-click the title bar, or
   press **Alt+Space**.
2. Choose **Arrange windows...**.
3. Choose **Left half**, **Right half** or **Maximize**. The outline previews the
   selected layout. Choose **Cancel** or press Escape to keep the old layout.
4. After choosing a half, select a suitable window for the other side. Choose
   **Keep the other side unchanged** or press Escape to skip this optional step.

You can also drag a title bar to a side edge or use **Windows+Left/Right**.
An outline appears during the drag; release to apply, or press Escape to cancel.
**Windows+Up** maximizes. **Windows+Down** restores normal bounds; on an already
normal window it minimizes. The title-bar Restore button also restores bounds.

Some apps need more than half the screen. Their half choices are disabled;
maximize them or use a wider desktop. Finish or cancel an app's file dialog
before rearranging that owner. Your unsaved text is kept when switching or
arranging. Closing still asks you what to do with unsaved work.

The desktop supports eight simultaneous windows. Close one if it explains that
limit. These controls do not add GPU acceleration or new hardware support.
