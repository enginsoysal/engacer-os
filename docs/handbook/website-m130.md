# Find your way around the Engacer OS documentation

The [documentation portal](https://engaceros.brixcanvas.com/blog/documentation) starts with what you want to do. Its current native instructions describe M12.18. M13.0 is a design program, not a newer installable OS.

1. **Start here:** understand current capabilities and read the desktop introduction.
2. **Install:** download the ISO or prepared VMware ZIP, follow installation, then check the matching tested scope.
3. **Use Engacer OS:** apps, windows, folders, file transfers, dialogs and USB removal.
4. **Customize:** supported Settings, keyboard/accessibility and distribution profiles.
5. **Project & development:** the current M13 plan, original concepts, architecture and contributing.
6. **Downloads:** the qualified native release and checksums; older downloads remain separately archived.

The [history and full reference](https://engaceros.brixcanvas.com/blog/documentation-archive) retains previous milestones and earlier technical/user documents. An old installation article does not become current because its publication date changed. Existing article URLs remain available.

## Search today and the proposed replacement

The live BrixCanvas **Search** link currently opens its search page. That remains the working fallback. The proposed same-page panel, Ctrl+K, current-guide ranking and archive opt-in are demonstrated in the M13.0 design package; they are not yet the production search. Renderer-side article sidebars and curated Previous/Next are also tracked M13.1 work. Current task-route pages supply an explicit ordered list while those templates are pending.

To review the proposed visuals, open the [M13 concept gallery](../architecture/desktop-m13.md). To follow delivery, read the [ordered milestone plan](../milestones/m13.md). Every native stage must publish its matching guide/downloads and verify the actual website before the next stage starts.
